Log in with your browser's login

Use this if Twitch makes you sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook and that won't work here.

  1. In Firefox, go to twitch.tv and make sure you're logged in.
  2. Press F12, open the Storage tab, then Cookies and https://www.twitch.tv.
  3. Find the row named auth-token and copy its value.

This value is like your password. Only paste it here, never send it to anyone.
